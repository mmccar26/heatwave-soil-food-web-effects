MANUSCRIPT

Title:
“Predator-prey interactions are disrupted under simulated heatwave conditions”

REPOSITORY DESCRIPTION

This repository contains the data and R scripts used to analyze how simulated
heatwave conditions, predation, and land-use history affect prey and predator
abundance in a soil microcosm experiment. The experiment compared microcosms
established with material from three land-use contexts (grassland, intensive
agriculture, and urbanized habitats) under ambient and simulated heatwave
temperature treatments. Collembola were used as prey and rove beetles
(Staphylinidae) as predators.

FILE DESCRIPTIONS

README.md
  Provides the manuscript title and author information.

Metadata.txt
  Describes the repository contents, data fields, and requirements for running
  the analyses.

Data/ambient_temp_final.csv
  Abundance data from the ambient-temperature treatment. The file contains 54
  observations: three replicate microcosms for each combination of three
  land-use contexts, two predation treatments, and three harvest weeks.
  Sampling dates were December 8, 15, and 22, 2023.

Data/heatwave_temp_final.csv
  Abundance data from the simulated-heatwave treatment. The file contains 54
  observations: three replicate microcosms for each combination of three
  land-use contexts, two predation treatments, and three harvest weeks.
  Sampling dates were February 9, 16, and 23, 2024.

Scripts/Fig-1-plot.R
  Reproduces Figure 1, which shows mean prey and predator abundance through
  time across the ambient and heatwave temperature treatments and the three
  land-use contexts. The figure includes standard errors, raw observations,
  initial abundances at week 0, treatment colors, and panels A–F. The script
  writes “arthropod_abundance_with_raw_data.png.”

Scripts/Fig-2-plot.R
  Reproduces Figure 2, which compares final Collembola abundance at week 3
  across predation, temperature, and land-use treatments. The script fits a
  factorial ANOVA, calculates estimated marginal means and Tukey grouping
  letters, displays raw observations and heatwave-associated percentage
  changes, and writes “week3_collembola_abundance_raw_data.png.”

Scripts/soil_predator_prey_analysis_FINAL.R
  Performs the primary analyses of prey and predator abundance across the full
  experiment and at week 3. The script calculates total predator abundance as
  the sum of adult and larval Staphylinidae, fits factorial ANOVA models, runs
  Tukey post-hoc comparisons, produces summary statistics and earlier figure
  versions, and exports Tukey comparison tables.

Scripts/soil_predator_prey_analysis_no-contamination-FINAL.R
  Contains the sensitivity-analysis workflow intended to exclude suspected
  Sminthuridae contamination from the heatwave dataset before repeating the
  full-experiment analyses. This script references columns from an earlier,
  more detailed version of the data that are not included in the two final CSV
  files currently stored in the repository. It therefore cannot be run with
  the repository’s final CSV files without modification or access to those
  earlier data.

DATA DICTIONARY

Both CSV files contain the same ten columns:

site
  Land-use context from which the experimental material originated.
  Values: “Grassland,” “Intensive Agriculture,” and “Urbanized.”

predation_trt
  Experimental predation treatment.
  “C” = Collembola prey without rove beetles (prey-only treatment).
  “CB” = Collembola prey with rove beetles (predator-addition treatment).

rep
  Replicate microcosm identifier within an experimental treatment combination.

harvest_week
  Week of destructive microcosm harvest. Values: 1, 2, and 3.

harvest_date
  Calendar date on which the microcosm was harvested, recorded as M/D/YYYY.

analyzer
  First name of the person who processed or counted the sample.

collembola
  Count of Collembola recovered from the microcosm. This is the primary prey
  abundance variable used in the analyses.

staphylinidae
  Count of adult rove beetles (family Staphylinidae) recovered from the
  microcosm.

staphylinidae_larval
  Count of larval rove beetles recovered from the microcosm.

sminthuridae
  Count of Sminthuridae recovered from the microcosm. This variable is retained
  to document potential contamination but is not included in the primary
  Collembola response variable in the final datasets.

EXPERIMENTAL DESIGN REPRESENTED IN THE DATA

Temperature treatments:
  Ambient
  Simulated heatwave

Land-use contexts:
  Grassland
  Intensive Agriculture
  Urbanized

Predation treatments:
  C  = prey only
  CB = prey + predator

Harvest weeks:
  1, 2, and 3

Replication:
  Three replicate microcosms per temperature × land-use context × predation
  treatment × harvest-week combination.

Initial conditions used by the figure code:
  Prey treatments began with 20 Collembola per microcosm.
  Predator-addition treatments began with 5 rove beetles per microcosm.
